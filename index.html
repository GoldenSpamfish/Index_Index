Custom Index Builder

Mix and match any combination of global indicators, calibrate weights with responsive sliders, and choose your preferred aggregation mathematics (such as geometric means that penalize multidimensional deficits).

03

Correlation & Comparison Studio

Explore linear and non-linear relationships between your custom index and standard international benchmarks, calculating Pearson \(r\), Spearman rank correlation \(\rho\), and polynomial regression curves.

04

Country & Regional Performance Anatomy

Diagnose why specific countries or regional archetypes (e.g. Nordic economies, East Asian Tigers, Latin America) excel or lag across index families, uncovering the structural collinearity behind international rankings.

05

2D Bivariate Matrix & Quadrant Field

Position every nation on a 2D matrix (e.g. Wealth across, Custom Index up) across 16 discrete tier combinations to identify aspirational outliers and structural trade-offs.

06

Sensitivity & Leave-One-Out Analysis

Test the robustness of your index: determine which sub-indicators account for the true statistical variance, how rankings shift if a metric is omitted, and map which nations benefit most from each indicator.

07

Data Studio & Sharing

Import your own custom datasets from CSV or JSON files, download composite rankings, or generate a permanent shareable URL containing your complete index specification.
